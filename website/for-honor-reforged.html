For Honor Reforged

Melee combat in Zenderal is directional. The way you are moving when you attack decides what the attack does, and breaking an enemy's poise opens them up for knockdowns and finishers.

Poise: the yellow bar

Every fighter has poise as well as health.

The yellow bar under an enemy's health starts full and drains with every hit. Heavy weapons, power attacks and parries drain the most, and armour reduces it. A single big hit makes them stagger. When the bar is empty they are open for a knockdown. Stop hitting them for a few seconds and it refills.

You have poise too, so blocking and parrying are about protecting your own bar as much as their health.

Directional attacks

Hold a direction while you attack. Forward or no direction gives your weapon's normal attacks. Third person only.

Back + Light attack

Parry

A counter-swing. Time it to meet an incoming hit: you take no poise damage and less damage, and you hit their poise hard. If a parry empties their poise, they are knocked down. Your Block skill makes parries stronger.

Back + Power attack

Guardbreak

Weak damage at half stamina cost. Its job is to set the enemy up. If they're blocking, their guard breaks and you can follow up. If they're not blocking, they become Unstable.

Left / Right + Attack

Side attacks

Cheaper, lighter hits for keeping a combo going and chipping away poise. They also strip an enemy's empowered attack on that side (see the arrows below).

Power attack on a downed enemy

Followup

After any knockdown or guardbreak, a power attack becomes a leaping finisher. It costs half stamina and deals bonus damage that grows with Block.

Knockdowns and status icons

These icons appear on the enemy you're targeting. A knockdown leaves a 10-second debuff, and they can't be knocked down the same way again until it ends.

Unstable

Hit by a guardbreak while not blocking. Empty their poise now to knock them down.

Stagger knockdown

Poise broken while Unstable. Their armour is lowered for 10 s.

Parry knockdown

Poise broken by a parry. Their damage is lowered for 10 s.

Guardbreak

Guard broken while blocking. Their blocking is weakened for 10 s.

Shield flip

Knocked down by a shield counter. Can't happen again until the icon clears.

Enemy empowered attacks

Enemies power up attacks from the left or right during a fight. An arrow on that side of their bar warns you.

White: light, drains your staminaYellow: heavy, deals extra damageOrange: both, with extra bonuses

Left light

Their left light attack drains your stamina.

Right light

Their right light attack drains your stamina.

Left heavy

Their left heavy attack deals extra damage.

Right heavy

Their right heavy attack deals extra damage.

Left, both

Light and heavy on the left at once.

Right, both

Light and heavy on the right at once.

Strip them: hit the enemy with the matching attack, for example a left light attack against a white left arrow. This doesn't work while they're blocking unless you've guardbroken them first. If you let an enemy collect all four, they get a large damage boost.

Three combos to learn

  1. Against a blocker: guardbreak, then power attack for the followup.
  2. Against an aggressive enemy: parry their swings until their poise breaks, then power attack.
  3. Against an open enemy: guardbreak to make them Unstable, chain side attacks until their poise breaks, then power attack.
