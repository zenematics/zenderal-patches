# TraitsRework

- **Source:** `src/TraitsRework/` — generated by `tools/01-build-traits.py`; **never hand-edit** `Spells/`, `MagicEffects/`, `Perks/`, `FormLists/`
- **Ships:** `Zenderal - Traits.esp` (ESL, HEDR 1.7; masters Skyrim, Update, FS, `Biggie Traits.esp`)
- **Requires:** Biggie Traits - Enderal Conversion (the picker menu). Zen Traits' 9 traits still reach the menu through its own `Zen Traits_FLM.ini`
- **FormID block:** `0x800–0x88F` — trait *n* (0-based, in generator order) uses `0x800+4n` SPEL, `+1` MGEF, `+2` PERK, `+3` spare (`0x800–0x846` used); shared stat MGEFs `0x870–0x878`; leveled lists `0x880–0x88F` (`0x880–0x882` used)
- **Owns:** `Traits_AbilityList 000002:Biggie Traits.esp` (no other list plugin overrides it; only Zen Traits masters Biggie, and it adds through FLM)
- **Status:** pass 1 built 2026-10-21: the 16 [PE]/[Cond] traits. The Spriggit round trip is byte-identical,
  all 68 emitted FormKeys resolve against the masters, and `build.ps1` packages it. **Not loaded in xEdit,
  not verified in game.** Feverborn, Duelist and Bloodthirst ([Script]) and Iron Stomach (deferred, see below) are not built.

## Gunslinger (trait #18, added 2026-10-23)

The curator's design: "Flintlock pistols deal 35% more damage, and people you kill have a 30% chance to carry Flintlock
Bullets. Your melee attacks deal 25% less damage." Trait 18 → `0x844` SPEL, `0x845` MGEF, `0x846` PERK; loot list `0x882`.
- **Pistols are staves firing a spell** ([`FlintlockOverhaul`](FlintlockOverhaul.md)): their damage is the shot
  enchantment's magnitude, so a weapon `ModAttackDamage` never touches them. The bonus is `ModSpellMagnitude` ×1.35 on
  spell keyword `ZP_FlintlockShot 00080C:Zenderal - Flintlock Overhaul.esp`, the same condition the overhaul's own
  scaling perk `ZP_FlintlockArcheryScaling 00080D` uses.
- **Melee** ×0.75: `ModAttackDamage` when the weapon (tab 1) lacks `WeapTypeBow 01E715`. Bows, crossbows and the Ordo
  muskets all carry it, so ranged is spared; fists count as melee.
- **Ammo:** `AddLeveledListOnDeath` (Goblin Slayer's shape) of `ZEN_TR_GunslingerLoot 882`: ChanceNone 70%, else 3 or 5
  `ZP_FlintlockBullet 000800:Zenderal - Flintlock Overhaul.esp`. Victims must have `ActorTypeNPC 013794` (people, not
  animals or creatures).
- **New master:** `Zenderal - Flintlock Overhaul.esp` (loads at 346, before Goblins and Traits).
- **Art:** the curator's `outputs/trait-icons/27-Gunslinger.png` → `tools/02` (now expects 27 icons) →
  `Interface/TraitPics/ZEN_TR_GunslingerAb.dds` (two smoking flintlock pistols, converted and deployed 2026-10-23).
- Built and deployed 2026-10-23 (previous plugin in `E:/Zenderal-backups/2026-10-23-traits-pre-gunslinger/`).
  Unverified in game.

## The trait menu needs `Traits_Utils.pex` on 1.7.104 (2026-10-21)

**Symptom:** the trait menu opened with no entries at all ("0 / 2 Items selected"), including Zen Traits' 9.

**Cause** **[verified]:** Biggie's `Traits_QuestScript.ShowTraitsMenu` fills the menu through two **native** functions
in `Traits_Utils`, `PopulateTraitsList` and `GetTraitsAtIndexes`, implemented in Biggie's `Traits.dll`. That DLL is
an old CommonLibVR build that can't read the 1.7.104 address library, so the migration hid it
(`Traits.dll.mohidden`; see [`enderal-update-migration.md`](../workspace/enderal-update-migration.md)). The natives
are unbound, so the menu opens empty. The list itself was fine: FormListManipulator.log shows Zen's 9 added
to `Traits_AbilityList [FE11A002]`.

**Fix:** `src/TraitsRework/Scripts/source/Traits_Utils.psc` is Biggie's script with those two functions rewritten
in Papyrus. Rows are added with B612's own `b612_TraitsMenu.AddItem(name, description, image)` (`b612.esp`
`0x803`; `B612.dll` loads on 1.7.104), and descriptions come from powerofthree's `GetDescription`.

**Trait images** (2026-10-21): Traits.dll's own convention, read from its strings, is `"TraitPics/" + EditorID + ".dds"`.
The B612 menu `loadMovie()`s that from `Interface/` into its details pane (`ItemPic_mc`), with no resize code in
the swf, so it shows at native size. `Traits_Utils` passes the same path, with EditorIDs from powerofthree's
Tweaks' editorID cache (its log: "Installed editorID cache"). `tools/02-build-icons.py` turns the curator's ChatGPT
art (`outputs/trait-icons/NN-<Name>.png`, gitignored, 1254² each) into `Interface/TraitPics/ZEN_TR_<Stem>Ab.dds`:
BC1/DXT1, no mips, committed because CI can't run texconv. Zen Traits' 9 have art too (`17-`…`25-*.png` →
`ZenTraits_<Stem>Ab.dds`; the generator hard-codes their EditorIDs from `Zen Traits_FLM.ini`). Zen's art ships in
*this* mod, so it only shows with Zenderal - Traits installed.
**Verified in game (2026-10-21):** DDS loads in this menu at native size. A 256 px test drew about 93 px in a
1741-wide screenshot (≈0.363 screen px per image px). Images are now **932×760**, sized to the pane the curator
marked: the square art is center-cropped (about 9% off top and bottom), never stretched. The 932×760 size is not yet seen in game. Picks come back
through a literal-size `Spell[]` ladder, since Biggie's MCM caps picks at 5. Every other `Traits_Utils` function stays
native and stays dead: Biggie's own skill-book, Master of One and spell-list traits. None of those are in our list.
No properties, so swapping the loose script is safe for saves. **Zenderal - Traits must outrank Biggie Traits in MO2.**
If `Traits.dll` is ever restored with a 1.7 build, drop this script: SKSE can't bind natives to functions that are no
longer declared native.

**Compile:** the `papyrus-compile` recipe still fails here (see [`PerkOverhaul.md`](PerkOverhaul.md#compiling)). What
works is absolute paths, with `-i=<source>;<papyrusSource.enderal>;<papyrusSource.skse>;B612 Source/Scripts;PE Source/scripts;PapyrusUtil AE SE/Scripts/Source`.

## Pass 1 — what was built, and where it differs from the design below

- **Our 16 abilities sit directly in the `Traits_AbilityList` override**, instead of an empty override plus our own
  `_FLM.ini`. The effect is the same: Biggie's traits leave the menu, and Zen Traits' FLM adds its 9 at runtime.
  It is one fewer runtime dependency, because FLM is only needed for Zen's traits.
- **HEDR 1.7, the same as `Biggie Traits.esp`.** Biggie is itself a 1.7 light plugin whose records sit below `0x800`
  (`000002`…), so a 1.7 override is read exactly as Biggie's own record is. The CLAUDE.md "must be 1.71" rule is
  for sub-`0x800` forms from a **1.71** plugin, which doesn't apply here. Worth checking that xEdit agrees.
- **Percent penalties to max Health/Stamina became flat**, because a pre-AE ability can't scale a max AV by a percentage:
  Overcharged −25 Health, Ink-Stained −25 Stamina, Brewer's Lungs −15 Health. Retune after play.
- **Heavy Hand:** "sprinting costs 20% more" became **power attacks cost 20% more Stamina** (`ModPowerAttackStamina`), because no entry point or AV scales sprint drain.
- **Cold-Hardened:** the "Stamina drains slower" clause was dropped. It is now frost resist 30 / fire weakness 20.
- **Nightwalker / Back-Alley Born:** the sneak bonus is `ModDetectionSneakSkill` **+20 / +15 skill**, not a percentage.
- **Iron Stomach is deferred.** Whether `ModSpellMagnitude`/`ModSpellDuration` apply to *consumed* ingestibles is
  unverified, and guessing risks an inert trait. It needs a test perk first, or an `OnItemEquipped` script.
- **#4 and #5 are not mutually exclusive.** Entropic Pact's duration bonus excludes summon/reanimate spells (via
  the summon keywords), so the two don't stack on summons.

### Test checklist (in game)

Each needs a check that the effect actually happens, not just that the trait shows in the menu:

1. The trait menu lists exactly our 16 + Zen's 9.
2. Ark-Born / Back-Alley Born: does `GetInCurrentLoc` count child locations (an Ark shop interior)?
3. Pathfinder: `SpeedMult` toggling on a conditioned ability needs a carry-weight change to refresh. Does the speed change show up?
4. Steady Breath: `IsMoving` is evaluated when the projectile hits, not when it's fired.
5. Heavy Hand: does `IsPowerAttacking` still fire under FHR/MCO power attacks?
6. Iron Will: do Mind Wrack and Mesmerize land at half strength?
7. Ink-Stained: is a handbook from a vendor 25% cheaper?

Twenty Enderal-specific traits to replace Biggie Traits' Skyrim-flavoured list. Each trait has one
upside and one downside, and the downside works against the trait's own playstyle. Numbers sit in
the range Enderal's own class perks use (10–30%).

## How Biggie's picker works (what we plug into)

**[verified]** from `Biggie Traits - Enderal Conversion/Source/Scripts/Traits_QuestScript.psc`:

- The menu (`b612_TraitsMenu`) lists every **Spell** in the FormList `Traits_AbilityList` and shows
  that spell's name and description. Picking a trait runs `PlayerRef.AddSpell(spell)`. A trait is
  therefore one **ability SPEL** whose effects carry the behaviour.
- The number of picks comes from MCM: `iMaxTraits:Main` (default **2**, `MCM/Config/Biggie Traits/settings.ini`)
  and `iRequiredTraits:Main`.
- **Zen Traits already adds its 9 traits without touching Biggie's plugin**, through
  `Zenderal - Patch Collection/Zen Traits_FLM.ini` (`FormList = Traits_AbilityList|ZenTraits_*Ab`).
  That is the archetype to copy.

### Plan

1. New ESL `Zenderal - Traits.esp` holding the 20 ability SPELs, their MGEFs, and the PERKs they add
   (perk entry points are the cheap, reliable route; see the tags below).
2. `Zenderal - Traits_FLM.ini` adds the 20 abilities to `Traits_AbilityList`.
3. **"Rework completely"** means removing Biggie's own traits from the menu as well. Two options, to decide:
   - **(a)** override `Traits_AbilityList` in our ESL with an empty list. FLM then adds back only what the
     inis list (ours + Zen Traits'). Our plugin **owns** that FLST.
   - **(b)** keep Biggie's list and only add ours (40+ entries in the menu).
4. Several Biggie traits have quest/player-alias scripts (Homeowner, Witcher, Angler…). Removing them from the
   list stops new picks; existing saves keep the ability. That is harmless, but test it on a save that has one.

## The 20 traits

Tags: **[PE]** a perk entry or a stat change only. **[Cond]** a perk entry plus a condition, record side
verified above. **[Script]** needs Papyrus. **[Test]** an engine behaviour that has not been checked in Enderal.

### Magic

**1. Feverborn**: you gain Arcane Fever 25% more slowly, but all healing you receive is 20% weaker.
- Fever: add one `HasPerk` branch to `ZenTraits_ArcaneFeverScript.ApplyIncreaseFeverMods`, mirroring Mental
  Expert's ×0.67. This script is Zen Traits' (MIT), and [`ZenTraitsFixes`](ZenTraitsFixes.md) already forks
  Zen Traits, so fork it there. **[Script]**
- Healing: `Mod Incoming Spell Magnitude` ×0.8 when the effect has `MagicRestoreHealth 01CEB0`. **[Cond][Test]**
  Do potions go through this entry point?
- ⚠ Overlaps with Zen Traits' **Purified Blood**, which already multiplies fever gain. Either drop one or make sure they stack sensibly.

**2. Overcharged**: Elementalism deals 20% more damage, but your maximum Health is 15% lower. **[PE]**
`Mod Spell Magnitude`, condition `EPMagic_SpellHasSkill Destruction`.

**3. Stormcaller**: shock spells deal 25% more damage and cost 10% less; fire and frost spells deal 15% less. **[Cond]**
Keywords `01CEAF` / `01CEAD` / `01CEAE`. **Also require school = Destruction**, or the fire penalty hits the 26 holy-fire Light Magic effects.

**4. Entropic Pact**: Entropy costs 20% less and its curses last 25% longer; **Light Magic** costs 25% more. **[Cond]**
*(Draft said "Vitalism", which does not exist. Healing is Light Magic, so the tension holds.)*

**5. Gravecaller** *(was "Phasmalist's Pupil")*: summons and Revive last 50% longer; undead deal 15% more damage to you. **[Cond]**
Duration: `Mod Spell Duration` on `MagicSummonFamiliar 1091CF` / `MagicSummonUndead 02482B`.
Damage: `Mod Incoming Damage` with attacker `HasKeyword ActorTypeUndead 013796`.
Phasmalism is a class in Enderal, not a school; the summons are Entropy. ⚠ It shares a school with #4, so the two shouldn't both be picked. Consider making them mutually exclusive.

**6. Iron Will**: you resist **Psionics** (fear, mesmerise, mind wrack) by 50%; blocking absorbs 15% less damage. **[Cond]**
*(Draft said Mentalism, but Mentalism is shields and Blink.)* `Mod Incoming Spell Magnitude` with `EPMagic_SpellHasSkill Illusion`.

### Melee and ranged

**7. Cornered Wolf**: below 30% Health you deal 25% more damage and take 10% less; above 75% you deal 10% less. **[Cond]** `GetActorValuePercent Health`.

**8. Heavy Hand**: power attacks deal 30% more damage; normal attacks deal 10% less; sprinting costs 20% more Stamina. **[Cond][Test]**
`Mod Attack Damage` + `IsPowerAttacking`. FHR/MCO in this list change power-attack handling, so test that the condition still fires.

**9. Duelist**: +15% damage with one enemy, −15% with three or more. **[Script]** It needs a count of the enemies in combat (PO3 or a cloak scan). The most expensive trait; cut it first if scope is tight.

**10. Steady Breath**: bow and crossbow shots fired while standing still deal +20%, while moving −15%. Covers muskets. **[Cond][Test]** Which condition reliably reads "standing still" for the player?

**11. Bloodthirst**: kills restore 10% Health; out-of-combat Health regeneration is halved. **[Script]** It needs a kill event (Story Manager kill node or a PO3 event).

### Survival and world

**12. Nightwalker**: from 20:00 to 05:00, +20% sneak and +25% Mana regeneration; −10% Stamina regeneration during the day. **[Cond]** `GameHour 000038`.
⚠ Its theme is close to Zen Traits' **Night Fever**.

**13. Cold-Hardened**: 30% frost resistance and Stamina drains 10% more slowly; 20% weakness to fire. **[PE]**

**14. Iron Stomach** *(reworded)*: **Satiated** is twice as strong and you resist poison by 50%; potion effects last 15% shorter. **[Cond][Test]**
Food barely heals in Enderal, so the trait now boosts Satiated (`01ADF3`). Does `Mod Spell Magnitude` apply to ingestibles? If not, this needs an `OnItemEquipped` script.

**15. Wildling**: +15% damage dealt to and −15% taken from `ActorTypeAnimal`; prices 15% worse. **[Cond]**
"Animal" here includes trolls, chaurus, frostbite spiders and sabre cats. That's probably fine, but it's broader than the name suggests.

**16. Pathfinder**: out of combat and outdoors, +15% speed; carry weight −50. **[Cond]** `IsInInterior == 0` + `IsInCombat == 0`.

### Economy and social

**17. Ark-Born**: prices 15% better in Ark and Riverville; Health regenerates 25% more slowly in the wilderness. **[Cond]**
Use **explicit locations**, not keywords: `GetInCurrentLocation CapitalCityLocation 0A1A12` OR `FlusshaimLocation 032706`. Riverville has no `LocTypeCity`. [Test] Does the condition count child locations (shops, inns)?

**18. Back-Alley Born**: pickpocketing and lockpicking 20% easier, sneak +15% in the same two locations; buying costs 10% more everywhere. **[Cond]** Same location conditions as #17.

**19. Ink-Stained**: handbooks cost 25% less; maximum Stamina −15%. **[Cond]**
`Mod Buy Prices`, item `HasKeyword Lehrbuch 031ACD`. Decide whether the Memory book and talent books (same keyword) also get the discount. If not, add `GetIsID` exclusions.

**20. Brewer's Lungs**: potions you make are 20% stronger; maximum Health −10%. **[PE]** `Mod Alchemy Effectiveness`.
