## What it does

Curator's request (2026-11-01): "a reload mod for Flintlock pistols … that places the bullet in the barrel similar to
the musket". No such animation exists for Skyrim SE: every pistol pack on Nexus (Loop's, hatly's GLENMORIL/VIGILANT
OAR set, thenewkid20's blunderbuss, gaju's LE set, Gun Animations) covers only aim, fire, idle and movement, because
pistols are staves and a staff has no reload state. The only one-handed reload clip found was Spaghetti Western's
revolver (a crossbow). So this is **our own animation**, built procedurally with the musket reload's machinery
([`FlintlockOverhaul`](FlintlockOverhaul.md) tools/09–11).

After a shot with a pistol in the **left hand** and the **right hand empty**, third person: the pistol comes down in
front of the body, muzzle up; the right hand takes a ball from a belt pouch at the right hip, drops it into the muzzle,
draws the ramrod from under the barrel, rams it (one push, one short stroke), stows it and returns; the pistol goes
back up to Loop's idle. 2.67 s in all (the shot fires at 0.334 s; the Flintlock Overhaul script reloads the gun 2.0 s
after it, as the rod is stowed at 2.32 s). The head turns toward the muzzle while loading. The same, mirrored, for a
pistol in the **right hand** with the left hand empty (2026-11-02), and in **first person** for the left hand.

## Why it can live in the shot clip (magic behaviour graph, Pandora output, read 2026-11-01)

`StfMagic_Release` (clip generator `#0916`) sits in `MLh_AimedSpellReleaseState` of `MLh_AimedFireAndForgetBehavior`.
Its clip triggers: `MLh_SpellFire_Event` 0.334 s (the shot), `MLh_WinStart` 0.337 s, `MLh_WinEnd` 0.667 s,
`MLh_Equipped_Event` at the clip's end. The state's only transition, `MLh_SpellAimedStart -> PreCharge`, uses the
`WinStart`–`WinEnd` initiate interval; otherwise the state is left only at the clip's end. So a longer shot clip holds
the reload, exactly as Zodd's musket shot clip does in the 1hm graph.

Bone masks (which bones the clip drives): standing, `LeftHandMagicCast_MSG[iLeftHandType 8] ->
LHStaff_RHMagicCast_MSG[iRightHandType 0] -> MLh_ReadyCast_Behavior` has **no mask** (whole body); moving
(`MagicCast_UpperBody_BoneSwitchGen`), sneaking (`SneakMagicCast_BoneSwitchGen`) and turning take the **UpperBody**
mask (property names per graph: each behaviour has its own `characterPropertyNames`, not the character file's). With
a weapon in the right hand the sword arm would ram, so the submods require `IsEquippedType` right = 0 (OAR's unarmed,
as Beam Walking Assist uses it).

**But that is not the graph the player is in** (found with [`ZenBoneTrace`](ZenBoneTrace.md), 2026-11-01): a staff in
the left hand with the right hand empty is the **one-handed** state, `1hm_behavior` → `1HM_Readied_Staff_State` →
`1HM_MLh_Behavior` → `MLh_1HM_Idle_Blend`, a blend of the magic graph (`1HM_MagicBFR`: our clip) and the clip
`1HM_Idle` (the free hand's idle) with complementary bone weights (template `#4472` / `#4470`): our clip gets the whole
body except the right arm, where the right clavicle, upper arm and their twists are **half and half** and the right
forearm, hand, fingers, forearm twists, `AnimObjectR`, `Weapon` and `NPC R MagicNode` are **the idle's alone**. The
idle that wins is Goetia Enchanted Magic Staves' OAR submod *Unarmed Right Staff Left sprint* (priority 68527: drawn,
right hand unarmed, left hand staff), the same file as Goetia Magic Spell Casting's DAR 68522/68523. Traced: the right
forearm and hand matched that idle within 0.1° and barely moved (≤ 4°) through the reload; the right upper arm fitted a
50/50 blend of the idle and our clip (residual 1–3°). Vanilla does this so a free hand keeps its own idle while the
other hand casts.

**The free right arm (fix, 2026-11-01):** the Pandora patch's `1hm_behavior.xml` (`tools/03`) binds the `1HM_Idle`
child's weight to a new graph variable `fZenPistolRightArmIdle` (default 1.0; also declared in `0_master` so the
engine finds it by name from Papyrus) and raises the magic child's zero right-arm weights to 0.01. At 1 the free hand
looks as before (1% of the magic graph mixed in); at 0 the magic graph, i.e. our clip, owns the whole right arm. The
quest `ZP_PistolReloadArmQuest` (0x806, player alias) runs `ZP_PistolReloadArmScript`: on a player spell-fire from the
left hand with a pistol (Loop's keyword `801`, looked up by FormID), the right hand empty and no Hair Trigger, it sets
the variable to 0, and back to 1 2.1 s later (the newest shot owns the reset). Every frame of the clip gives the right
arm the pose the game showed before the switch (`Variant.rest`: clavicle, upper arm and their twists halfway
between the clip's own and the idle's, the rest the idle's most typical frame, rotation and scale; the idle sways
≤ 6.7°), and the reload's
right-arm motion starts and ends there, so the switch at either end is seamless whenever Papyrus gets to it.

Fan the Hammer (0.1 s reload) refills inside the re-cast window and cuts this clip as it does Loop's. Hair Trigger
plays its own, faster twin of every reload (`tools/07`, 2026-11-02; until then its holders kept Loop's plain shot,
`HasPerk 0xB12` negated, and a 1.2 s timer loaded the gun). Two pistols: the
right hand is not empty, Loop's plain shot.

## How the clip is built (`tools/01`)

- **From the hold** (since 2026-11-02; before, Loop's shot frames 0–16 were kept, to the top of his recoil): frames
  0–16 (`KEEP_FRAMES`, 0.555 s) are Loop's `staff_idle` frame 0, with the free arm on its rest (checked after
  writing). The reload's motion starts there, so the pistol leaves the hold straight for the load pose. The body
  stays on that idle frame, and the clip ends on it exactly ("end 0.00 off"). Once checked, `trim()` cuts those 16
  frames off: the written clip starts in motion, every beat lies `TRIM` (0.555 s) earlier, the single reload runs
  2.11 s and the two-pistol one 3.40 s. `LOOP_FD` (0.034667) is asserted against Loop's clip.
- **Load pose** (`load_pose()`): candidates over muzzle height (from the shoulders), distance ahead, tilt forward and
  sideways, roll about the bore, and how far up the pistol the left hand holds it (the 40.6-unit Battlestained
  pistols are held 24 up the barrel: at the grip the left arm can't hold the muzzle low enough for the right hand to
  reach the rod's top). The left hand is solved exactly; the 12 cheapest candidates get every right-hand beat solved
  and the least strained one that reaches wins. **Holding further up = the Shield node's own track slides the pistol
  down through the hand** (the pistol hangs on `Shield`, a child of the hand; Loop's clips animate that node too). The
  first build moved only the hand target, so the pistol rode 24 units above every target and the rod was drawn from
  the middle of the barrel.
- **Beats** (`targets()`, `BEATS`): pinch beats (pouch, over the muzzle, drop) place the thumb–index pinch point
  (fingers from vanilla `male\mt_idlehhleftexitfast` frame 27, tips 0.82 apart); fist beats (grab, drawn, over the
  bore, rammed, stroke, withdrawn, stowed) place the grip centre = the right hand's `Weapon` node (fingers from
  `1hm_staggeridle` frame 9) on the stowed rod's line (beside the barrel on its underside, the pistol's +X) or the bore.
- **Each beat solved for the least strain** (`solve_beat()`): elbow direction around the shoulder–wrist line × the
  fist's roll about the rod × which way up the fist holds it; cost = wrist bend + ½ roll (steep past 60°/80°) +
  elbow above the shoulder or across the chest + **face clearance** (arm and grip ≥ 15 from the middle of the face;
  the first build passed 4.7 from it while ramming) + **continuity** with the previous beat (0.5/deg of hand turn,
  0.25/deg of elbow), solved in order.
- **Between beats the hand's rotation against its forearm is blended** (not its world rotation): a world-space blend
  let the IK forearm roll its own way, 152° wrist rolls between two beats of ~50°. The arm is solved twice per frame.
  The wrist roll goes 2/3 : 1/3 onto `ForearmTwist1/2` (Loop's own split: 31.5° / 15.7° on his left wrist).
- Elbows ease from the pose's own into the solved ones and back over each transition (no pop at Loop's last frame or
  into his idle; a 0.15 s ease flung the right arm out sideways at 2.50 s).

Build checks (fail the build): every hand within 0.5 of its target (all 0.00), wrist bend ≤ 80° and roll ≤ 100°
(worst: left 57/73, right 51/81), face clearance ≥ 10 (worst 16 on the long pistol), Loop's frames unchanged, the end
on Loop's idle, the annotations as written, Havok's block header.

**Loop's clips scale `NPC R Hand` by 0.852 on every frame** (found 2026-11-02; vanilla's and Goetia's are 1.0), and the

off the bore. Now the free arm takes its scale from the idle it hands over to (`Variant.rest`), so the ramming hand is

## The right hand (`tools/01`, 2026-11-02)

A staff in the right hand with the left hand empty is `0_master`'s `H2H_MRh_IdleMSG`[0] =
`H2H_MRh_Idle_BoneSwitch_3rdP`: the magic graph everywhere but the `LeftArm` mask (clavicle to fingers, binary, no
half-weighted bones), which plays `1HM_Idle` (Goetia's *Unarmed L Staff R sprint* submod, priority 68528, wins). The
Pandora patch replaces that selector slot with a blender built like vanilla's one-handed one: the magic graph (the
LeftArm bones at 0.01) and `1HM_Idle` (its weight bound to `fZenPistolLeftArmIdle`), and the script zeroes that
variable on a right-hand shot exactly as it does the right arm's on a left-hand one.

The clip is **solved in a mirrored world** (`Variant`): Loop's right-hand shot and idle and Goetia's idle mirrored
left <-> right (`mirror_hkx`, the gun's 180° turn on `Shield`), so the solver sees the same left-hand problem; the
result is mirrored back (the turn on `Weapon`). A double mirror is the identity, scale tracks included (they follow
the pose to the partner bone since the `mirror_hkx` fix). What else the mirror leaves to us:
- **The gun's scale.** The solver works with the gun at its real scale (`gun_scale`: the muzzle, barrel radius and
  rod scaled, the Shield offset and slide in world units) and asserts the right hand's is `RIGHT_GUN_SCALE` (1.0 since
  the fix; the first right-hand build kept Loop's 85% gun, with rods cut short for it). An old right-hand Loop set
  still installed fails the build.
- **The pistol mesh** in the mirrored `Shield` frame is z-flipped (`MESH_FLIP`): measured, the map from the real
  `Weapon` frame to the mirrored `Shield` frame is diag(1, 1, −1) (× 0.852 with the old right-hand set).
- **`AnimObjectR`/`L` are their own partners** in `mirror_hkx`, so each lands at its own reflection, hanging from the
  other side's hand (32 units off at the end). Both tracks are rebuilt from Loop's right-hand clips; over the prop
  frames the props move onto **`AnimObjectL`** (reflected: position S·p, rotation S·R·S, which keeps the rod on the
  reflected bore) and the annotations name the `AnimObjectL` records `ZEN_PistolBallL` / `ZEN_PistolRodL_<barrel>`
  (a mesh's `Prn` picks its node, so a prop serves one hand).

Checked in the real world (`check_right`): Loop's right-hand frames kept on the gun side, the end on his right idle,
the free left arm on Goetia's within 1.5°, ball and rod on the real bore line at the drop, the ram and the stroke
(≤ 0.5 units, rod axis parallel), the annotations. The frames between beats can still break a wrist the beats don't
(right-hand pistool03: a 130° roll), so `build_clip` falls through the load-pose candidates until the finished clip
stays within the wrist and face limits (pistool03 takes candidate 5). OAR submods `Reload <barrel> (right hand)`,
priority 1000021000 + i, with Loop's right-hand aim clips copied with `AnimObjectUnequip`.

## First person (`tools/04`, left hand, 2026-11-02)

First person plays **vanilla's staff clips** for the pistols (no list mod replaces the first-person `stfmagic_*`,
`staff_idle` or their right-hand twins). The first-person magic graph's `StfMagic_Release` has the third person's
triggers (fire 0.334 s, the state left only at the clip's end), so the longer clip holds the reload here too.
`tools/04` retargets each third-person left-hand clip with the musket's first-person `Retarget`
([`FlintlockOverhaul`](FlintlockOverhaul.md) tools/10): everything under Spine2 mapped through the eye with the
first-person bone lengths, both hands solved exactly onto the mapped hands; head, camera and the bones above Spine2
stay vanilla's. The clip is vanilla's 0.67 s shot to 0.40 s (sampled by time: Loop's clips run at 28.8 fps, vanilla's
at 30), blended into the reload by 0.72 s (before the ball loads), and into vanilla's `staff_idle` over the last
0.34 s, ending on it.

**The arms are lifted per barrel** so the muzzle sits 14° below the view's centre at the ram (`MUZZLE_BELOW`; +29 to
+35°). With only the head's look-down as lift (the musket's rule) the whole loading happened below the screen
(rendered from `Camera1st`, 2026-11-02); lifted, pistol, ball, rod and fist are in view from 0.72 s to the stow.

The free right arm in first person is not `1HM_Idle`: `1stP_MLh_1HM_Idle_Blend` gives the `RightArm` mask to
`1HM_1stPUpperBody`, the first-person upper-body locomotion, whose standing unarmed idle is SIGMA Fisting's `h`
(`h2h_idle`, priority 1000010) for a staff left and an empty right hand. The clip starts and ends on that idle's
least-swaying frame (**unverified**: no first-person trace yet; its hand is in view, so a wrong guess shows as a small
jump when the arm is handed over). The Pandora patch's first-person half (`firstperson~1hm_behavior`) appends the
weight binding to that child's existing binding set (its bone weights are bound to the `RightArm` character property)
and gives the magic child 0.01 on the 27 `RightArm` bones. OAR: `Reload 1P <barrel>` (first-person folder), the
third-person submod's priority and conditions, vanilla's first-person aim clips copied with `AnimObjectUnequip`.

**The right hand** (2026-11-02, after the scale fix): the same, from the third-person right-hand clips, on vanilla's
first-person `stfmagicright_release` (fire 0.341 s, also held to the clip's end) and `staffright_idle`. Its free left
arm is `0_master`'s `H2H_MRh_1stPUpperBody`, whose standing idle for an empty left hand is `H2H_Idle` too

## Props (`tools/02`) and the Pandora patch

Ball (radius 1.0, the musket ball's dark steel) and one ramrod per barrel (shaded like that pistol's barrel), hung on
**`AnimObjectR`** (the right hand rams), drawn and dropped by `AnimObjLoad.<EditorID>` / `AnimObjDraw.<EditorID>` /
`AnimObjectUnequip` annotations, as the musket's. The rod is laid on the bore (or the stowed line) through the fist's
grip centre, whichever way up the fist holds it. The mesh writer is the musket's (`FlintlockOverhaul/tools/11`
`write_prop`, which takes a `prn` attach bone since 2026-11-01).

**The magic behaviour lists none of the three events** (the 1hm graphs do; Pandora output, 2026-11-01), and an
annotation only fires if its graph lists the event. Pandora patch `zpistolreload` appends them to `magicbehavior`'s
string data `#0077/eventNames` and graph data `#0079/eventInfos` (node names from Pandora's template, converted with
`EnderalPairedAnims/tools/hkxconv --meta`). Without it the reload still plays, only without props. First person's
magic graph lacks them too (`firstperson~magicbehavior.xml`).

**First-person patch files** are named after Pandora's pack files, `firstperson~<graph>.xml`: Pandora names every graph
`<project>~<file>` (its `Engine.log`: `PackFile "firstperson~0_master"`, `defaultfemale~magicbehavior`), and a plain
`0_master.xml` reaches the third-person one. **Unconfirmed that Pandora's native patches resolve the prefixed name**
(the next `Engine.log` / a converted first-person output shows it: `fZenPistolRightArmIdle` in
`_1stperson/behaviors/1hm_behavior.hkx`).

Every submod also carries copies of Loop's six aim clips with `AnimObjectUnequip` at 0, so a reload cut short (dodge,
stagger) drops its prop when the gun is raised again; every shot clip starts with it too.

## Dual wielding (2026-11-02)

Curator's choice: **tuck and ram, one gun at a time.** After a shot with a pistol in each hand, the other hand puts its
loaded gun in the belt, does the ball and rod for the fired gun, and takes its own gun back.

**The graph** (third-person `magicbehavior`, Pandora's template; first person has the same nodes and IDs): two staves
take `LeftHandMagicCast_MSG` (standing, moving and sneaking alike) → `LHStaff_RHMagicCast_MSG`[`iRightHandType` 8] →
**`DualStaff_Blend` (#0623)**, for a cast from either hand. A parametric blend (flags 16) on `CastBlendDamped`:
- −1: `RHStaff_MLh_Favor_BoneSwitch`: the left hand's graph (`MLh_ReadyCast_Behavior`) with the `RightArm` mask
  (clavicle to fingers, `Weapon` included) on the right hand's (`MRh_ReadyCast_Behavior`);
- 0: `Equal_Magic_Blend`: the left arm from the left hand's graph, the right arm from the right's, spine, neck and
  head half and half (its bone weights #0594/#0596);
- +1: `LHStaff_MRh_Favor_BoneSwitch`, the mirror.

**Traced** (ZenBoneTrace 1.1.0, 14 shots with two Guardian pistols, 2026-11-02): at a shot `CastBlendDamped` is −1 for
the left gun and +1 for the right (0 when both guns were being aimed); `CastBlend` drops to 0 about 0.03 s later and
the damped value follows within ~0.35 s, so between shots the guns rest on the Equal blend. At the shot the other
arm is exactly Loop's idle for that hand at frame 0 (0.1–0.2°). `CastStop` (~0.55 s after a plain shot) then leaves
the casting state for a two-staff resting pose that matches no single clip, but the graph eases into it on its own,
as after any plain shot.

**The patch** (`tools/03`): the selector's slot 8 becomes a parametric blender on the new variable
