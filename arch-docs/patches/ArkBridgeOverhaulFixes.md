# ArkBridgeOverhaulFixes

- **Source:** `src/ArkBridgeOverhaulFixes/`, generated by `tools/01-build-ark-bridge-overhaul-fixes.py`
- **Ships:** `Zenderal - Ark Bridge Overhaul Fixes.esp`
- **FormID block:** `0x800–0x817` (book, four waterfall curtains, 19 water tiles)
- **Load after:** `KataHiddenPowers.esp` (mastered, along with `Ark Bridge Overhaul.esp`)

Fixes three things that `Ark Bridge Overhaul.esp` 1.5.1 (mod *Ark City Bridge -
Core*, Nexus 891) breaks: two items left unreachable and a bare waterfall drop. On 2026-10-18 this plugin started as *Ark Bridge Hidden
Powers Patch* and was renamed before its first release, when the second fix joined it.

## 1. Grappling Hook (Tier II) book

Reported 2026-10-18: "Grappling hook book tier 2 impossible to get". The book is KataPUMB Hidden
Powers' `Kata_P07_Hook_Book02` (**ALCH** `006D00:KataHiddenPowers.esp`, *[Warrior Tactics]
Grappling Hook (Tier II)*, renamed by EGO SE - KataPUMB Hidden Powers). Its only placement is REFR
`007985:KataHiddenPowers.esp` in Vyn cell `001FAB` (-5, 0) at (-18523.5, 24.2, 1577.9). Nothing
else references that ref (grep of the serialized Kata + EGO-Kata plugins). **[verified]**

Kata rested the book on Enderal's under-bridge crate `07C38C:Skyrim.esm` (`_00E_DungeonCrate02
07B824`, origin (-18547.6, 22.4, 1549.6): book is 28.3 units above it). ABO disables that whole
camp: the crate, its stack `07C38A`/`07C38B`, the broken crate, the barrels and the bedrolls all
get `InitiallyDisabled` and Z -30000. ABO also rebuilds the lower level at Z ≈ 1390. With the crate
gone the book had nothing under it. **[verified]** by a binary scan of every plugin in the `Ultra`
profile: ABO is the only plugin that disables pieces near the book, and Kata is the only plugin
that places anything on them. ABO's page lists only EPPI and Landscape Fixes as compatible, and
both of those patches are already in the list. No upstream Kata patch exists.

The fix:

- Kata's ref `007985` is disabled the xEdit way: `InitiallyDisabled`, enable parent PlayerRef
  (opposite), and Z -30000.
- A new copy, `000800`, of the same book `006D00` is placed in cell `001FAC` (-5, -1) on ABO's
  own replacement `_00E_DungeonCrate02` `0300015F:Ark Bridge Overhaul.esp` (scale 0.97). It sits
  at the original lid offset scaled by 0.97, plus 0.5 units of lift so it drops onto the lid,
  and keeps Kata's rotation and player ownership.

The crate is in the neighbouring cell, so the book can't just be moved. Moving an override into
a different cell's group is not a safe override, so the patch disables the old ref and places a
new one.

## 2. Magical Symbol `037889`

ABO's Nexus bug report *Magical Symbol inaccessible* (Juppsi, 2026-04-17, still "New issue") says
the bridge "completely blocks access" to Magical Symbol `037889`. It suggests moving the symbol to
(-15529.04, -1103.79, 1228.93), rotation Z 132.927°. **[community]**

The ref is `_00E_MagicalSymbol 03CAE2` (script `_00E_EPOnMagicalSymbolDis`: +1 to the
collected-symbols global, EP, and the 100-symbol achievement). It sits in `CapitalCityDoor
001FA5` (-4, -1) at (-15904, -455, 1295), on the riverbank 31 units above the terrain. Its
`LinkedReferences` is the light `_00E_MagicalSymbol_Light 03CAE4`, ref `03788C`. That ref is
persistent (in Vyn's persistent cell `001D40`), 10 units above the symbol, and the script disables
it on pickup. No quest references either ref, and no plugin in the list overrides either one.
**[verified]**

The cause: ABO adds `_00E_Ark_1024WallGate02 041448` as ref `0000E3:Ark Bridge Overhaul.esp` at
(-15693, -849, 1071), rotation Z 0.785 rad, **scale 1.4**. The winning mesh is the loose copy from
our ArkWallCurbFix, which is Enderal's original geometry. Its vertex bounds are x ±512, y ±256,
z −832…576. Moved into the gate's local frame (Bethesda Z rotation is clockwise from above), the
symbol lands at (−305, 93, 160): inside the box. Rays cast from the symbol against the mesh's
4962 triangles hit the gate in **all 14 directions** within 10–269 units, so the symbol is sealed
inside the wall. **[verified]** (geometry). No LAND edit is involved: the terrain at the symbol is
1264 in base Enderal, ABO, Landscape Fixes and the ABO Landscape patch alike.

The reporter's spot is open to the west and north (no hits toward −x, +y, 45°, 90°, 180°, 225°).
The wall is 38–65 units away on the other sides and a ledge 53 units overhead. Nothing is below for
268 units, and terrain is 189 units down, so the symbol floats beside the gate rather than inside
it. The reporter tested it in game; we have not.

The fix:

- `037889` is overridden in its own cell with the new position and rotation (2.320014 rad). Every
  other field, including the link to `03788C`, is copied verbatim.
- `03788C` moves with it, keeping its original offset (+4.12, +0.41, +9.66).

## 3. Bare waterfall drop under the first bridge

Found in game on 2026-10-18 from (-20010, -68, 1192): a straight-edged stretch with no water
under the first bridge. The river runs at water height 1280 in cell `001FAB` (-5, 0) and drops
off a cliff at the cell border (y ≈ 0) into cell `001FAC`. Enderal hides that edge with
`FXWaterfallBodySlope 01AC8C` curtains (scale 0.63, z 1223) and, lower down,
`FXWaterfallBodyTall 01AC8D` (scale 0.8). ABO moves the curtains either side of the first bridge's
pier (`_00E_Medieval_Ark_BridgeM 07C3F5`, x −19306) outward: slope `07C3F7` from −19613 to −19783,
slope `07C3F9` from −19019 to −18758, and the talls `07C3F6`/`07C3F8` likewise. Its new curtains
(`00000F`, `000011`, `000013`) are elsewhere along the drop, so about 1,000 units at the pier are
left bare. **[verified]** (positions); the cause of the in-game gap is inferred, not confirmed.

The fix: new copies `0x801–0x804` of those four refs, copied verbatim from base Enderal at their
original positions. ABO's moved originals are left alone (we don't override them), so the
curtains overlap slightly near each side of the pier.

In game (2026-10-18) the curtains helped, but a dry strip remained between the river's edge and
the foam crest under ABO's planks (screenshots from (-20003, -69, 1168), standing on
`RockCliff06` below the water line). The real cause is a seam base Enderal always had: the
river's surface is cell `001FAB`'s water (height 1280), so it stops dead at the cell border
y = 0, while the riverbed runs on south at ~1,000–1,100 to the falls. Ray-casting Enderal's own
stone bridge `07C3F5` shows nothing in that strip either (at x −20003 its mesh is all above
z 1459). It went unnoticed because Enderal's old walkway sat at ~1520; ABO's planks at z 1337
look straight at it. **[verified]** in game and against the meshes.

Two wrong turns, kept so nobody repeats them:

- A first top-down map (`.` = nothing between terrain and z 1300) counted ABO's arches and deck
  overhead as "cover" and found only one bare patch at x −19300, so one 256 × 256 water piece
  went there. The player's spot was 700 units west and unchanged. Cover overhead says nothing
  about water underneath.
- Restoring Enderal's curtain positions (`0x801–0x804`) adds foam at the pier but cannot fill
  the strip: the curtains start their fall south of it.

The fix: a row of 19 water tiles `0x805–0x817`, `Water512 0FD0CB` (water type
`DefaultWater 000018`, the same as Vyn's cell water) at scale 0.25 (128 × 128), z 1280, centred
on y −64 so they run from the cell border to y −128, just under ABO's plank edge (planks at 1337
stay dry). They abut each other and the river with no overlap, from x −20416 to −17984, the span
of ABO's bridge. Tiles that fall inside ABO's stone walls are simply hidden. **Confirmed
fixed in game 2026-10-18.**

## Cell and worldspace headers

- `001FAB`/`001FAC`: forwarded from the winner, which is Kata (it loads after ABO and both ABO
  patches). One exception: **Kata's `001FAC` drops `Location: CapitalCityLocation 0A1A12`**,
  which FS, EPPI, Landscape Fixes, ABO and both ABO patches all keep. This patch restores it.
- `001FA5` (overridden by FS, EPPI, Landscape Fixes, ArkCityGateConsistencyPatch, ABO, both ABO
  patches and Enderal - Donkey): base Enderal's header, cut before its LAND.
- Persistent cell `001D40` (overridden by ~45 list plugins; DynDOLOD's copy is an empty partial
  form): Kata's copy, carrying only `03788C`.
- Vyn WRLD: Kata's header.

Across every overriding plugin, these headers differ only in `XCLC` land-hide padding bits and the
order of the `XCLR` regions. The one exception is Kata's `001FAC` above. **[verified]** by binary
diff. The Vyn WRLD overrides differ only in the capitalisation of the HD LOD texture paths, except
Water for ENB's `NAM3`, which every later plugin already reverts (a pre-existing list issue,
separate from this patch).
