## What it does

A Gentleman's Flintlock Armoury (Enderal Nexus 378) ships its 8 pistols as **staves** firing `PMPistolShot 000802` (flat 100–300, soul-gem recharged). `800` `ZP_FlintlockBullet` (Ordo's ball mesh), `801` recipe (1 iron → 5), plus an override of Ordo's `OLMRecipeMusketBalls 00CE18` (Musket Balls `002F9D`): 2 iron → 10 (the winning Muskets-of-the-Enderal recipe was 2 iron + 1 steel → 3; the Imperial Muskets one, 1 iron → 20, loses in MO2), `802` pistol FormList, `803` StartGameEnabled quest + PlayerRef alias, `804–80B` per-pistol shot enchantments, `80C` keyword `ZP_FlintlockShot`, `80D` hidden perk `ZP_FlintlockArcheryScaling`, `80E–813` `ZP_DeathItem_*` UseAll lists, `814` `ZP_LootGunAmmo` (pick-one: Musket Balls ×10/×15, Flintlock Bullets ×5/×10). **Chest loot** (`tools/07`, 2026-10-15): one `ZP_LootGunAmmo` entry is appended to each of Enderal's 14 tiered chest-loot lists, where its arrows live — `01E–04E` `BeuteGering/Klein`, `BeuteMittel`, `BeuteGross/Gro`, and `01E/02E_Kampfitems`. Each override is EGO's record verbatim (EGO wins all 14 in a live load-order scan). `02E_Kampfitems 085667` was won by Ordo's base-Enderal copy, which reverted EGO's version (ChanceNone 0.15, extra arrows, 4 items), so it is now EGO's version plus Ordo's three additions (Musket Balls ×10/×15, Old Flintlock Musket `00F5E9`). **This plugin owns these 14 lists**; rerun `tools/07` after any EGO update. **Script:** SKSE `OnActorAction` type 2 (Spell Fire fires for staves, `slot` = hand) empties the hand; 2 s later one bullet refills it; equip loads from a bullet, unequip refunds, `InventoryMenu` close strips soul-gem charge. **Writing `Left/RightItemCharge` [verified in game 2026-10-12]:** `RestoreActorValue` only returns DAMAGED charge, so a gun at a hard 0 never reloads; from 0, Right took `SetActorValue`, Left ignored it and only took `ForceActorValue` — the script tries Restore → Set → Force, re-reading each time. **Damage** (`tools/01`): magnitude = **1.2** × the pistol's Damage stat (Archery-50 value; puts each pistol just under the Ordo-Enderal musket of its tier — muskets are weapon 6–35 + ammo 6/10/8, about bow level); perk applies ×0.5·(1+Archery×0.02), Fortify Archery, Strong Bowstring I/II, Concentration, Expert Locksmith and EGO's bow bonus with EGO's conditions (bow talents are `ModAttackDamage` on `WeapTypeBow` and never touch a staff spell). **NPCs** (`tools/00-scan-overriders.py` = reusable master-aware binary scan of the live load order → `overriders.json`; `tools/04-rebuild-npcs.py`): Ordo Legionis (Muskets of the Enderal, Nexus 525 — ships its OWN `OrdoLegionisMuskets.esp`, replacing the Skyrim one by filename; same FormIDs) overrides 95 NPCs, the flintlock pack 17, Enderal SE - Crossbows 8 of Ordo's, all as **base-Enderal copies** that reverted EGO's levels/skills/perks/AI/outfits on 82+10. 111 rebuilt = previous non-gun winner verbatim (EGO 92, FS 9, Skyrim 7, our UCR patch 2, EGO-Kata 1) + each gun mod's inventory delta vs the record it copied (Crossbows wins its 8, so Ordo's delta is skipped there); every second hostile musket NPC (Bandit/Nehrimes/Soeldner, sorted) gets a low pistol (Flintlock/Ancient ≤ level 10, else Priest's) + 5–10 bullets instead; 6 non-human carriers (Steel Watcher, Pahtira Centurion, skeleton miner, Miskahmur, Lyguria, Navigator) drop the pistol via a death list instead of wielding it; the porter's 7 deleted-pistol entries are dropped; orphan `02DE35:FS` "Gravewarden" (not a record in FS) skipped. **Sound** (`tools/02`): Ordo's own sounds; only `OLM_WPNBiggunFire 0197F7` / `OLM_WPNBlunderbussFire 00F5B8` re-pointed from the stub `01686B:Dawnguard.esm` output model to `10E425:Skyrim.esm`. **DAR Rifles** (`tools/05`): its anims require `IsEquippedRightHasKeyword("DAR Rifles.esp" | 0x800-0x802)`; the `_KID.ini` (archive root) adds `WeapTypeRifleBoltAction` to Ordo's 14 muskets; the separate ESL hides its 54 modern-gun recipes (AK-47…blasters) with `Smithing >= 1000`, no Items (originals reference Dawnguard). **First-person aim snap** (`tools/06`, MO2 mod `Zenderal - Musket Aim Fix`, clips not committed): the first-person sequence (OAR animation log 2026-10-14) is `CrossBow_IdleHeld` (hip, vanilla) → `CrossBow_Aim` (the RAISE) → `CrossBow_IdleDrawn` (the aimed HOLD, DAR) → `CrossBow_Release` (DAR). DAR's first-person `432101`/`432102` ship no `crossbow_aim`, so vanilla's plays: **0.2 s, 7 frames**, ending with the right arm ~100°, right hand ~150° and the `Weapon` bone 18 units off DAR's hold pose, which the graph closes in a quick blend just before the shot (first person only; third-person `432101` has its own aim). **v1** shipped the clips DAR parked in `432102/dlc01/New folder` — **byte-identical to vanilla**, no change. **v2** used DAR's hold clip as the raise — the gap just moved into the state blend, no change. **v3** (0.5 s synthesised raise) played slower, then still jumped: the first-person `1hm_behavior` clip generator `CrossBow Aim` (and Sneak/Dwarven) carries a **behaviour trigger `bowDrawn` at absolute 0.200 s**, and `CrossBowDrawing → CrossBow_IdleDrawn` fires on it with `fastBlendTransition` — **the raise is cut at 0.2 s whatever its length, so its 0.2 s pose must already be the hold**. A slower raise needs a behaviour patch moving that trigger (delaying fire for every first-person crossbow). **v4:** an OAR submod (keyword `0x800`, priority 432200) with a synthesised raise of exactly 0.2 s per variant: vanilla raise frame 0 (≈ the hip pose) smoothstep-eased into DAR's hold frame 0, keeping vanilla's annotations; read/written with **PyNifly's `hkx/anim_skyrim.py`, which reads and writes SE animation HKX without Blender** (round-trip exact; it decodes vanilla `sneakcrossbow_aim` track 41 `AnimObjectR` translation/scale as NaN — the tool starts that bone from the hold). v4 built 2026-10-14, not yet verified in game. **Holster: tried and reverted 2026-10-12.** Pistol meshes carry `Prn = SHIELD` (sheathed on the back); copies re-pointed at XPMSSE's `WeaponDaggerBackHip` did not give the wanted result in game, so they were removed — the curator places them with **Immersive Equipment Displays** instead. (PyNifly 29.1 cannot write `NiStringExtraData`, `NYI ... type 64`; the edit was a header string-table rewrite.) **Wheeler icons** (`tools/03`): `SKSE/Plugins/wheeler/resources/icons_custom/FID_<plugin>_0X<local FormID, no padding>.svg` (per-keyword variant `KWD_<EditorID>.svg`), curator's art.
