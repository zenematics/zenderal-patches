## What it does

A Gentleman's Flintlock Armoury (Enderal Nexus 378) ships its 8 pistols as **staves** firing `PMPistolShot 000802` (flat 100–300, soul-gem recharged). `800` `ZP_FlintlockBullet` (Ordo's ball mesh), `801` recipe (1 iron → 5), `802` pistol FormList, `803` StartGameEnabled quest + PlayerRef alias, `804–80B` per-pistol shot enchantments, `80C` keyword `ZP_FlintlockShot`, `80D` hidden perk `ZP_FlintlockArcheryScaling`, `80E–813` `ZP_DeathItem_*` UseAll lists. **Script:** SKSE `OnActorAction` type 2 (Spell Fire fires for staves, `slot` = hand) empties the hand; 2 s later one bullet refills it; equip loads from a bullet, unequip refunds, `InventoryMenu` close strips soul-gem charge. **Writing `Left/RightItemCharge` [verified in game 2026-10-12]:** `RestoreActorValue` only returns DAMAGED charge, so a gun at a hard 0 never reloads; from 0, Right took `SetActorValue`, Left ignored it and only took `ForceActorValue` — the script tries Restore → Set → Force, re-reading each time. **Damage** (`tools/01`): magnitude = **1.2** × the pistol's Damage stat (Archery-50 value; puts each pistol just under the Ordo-Enderal musket of its tier — muskets are weapon 6–35 + ammo 6/10/8, about bow level); perk applies ×0.5·(1+Archery×0.02), Fortify Archery, Strong Bowstring I/II, Concentration, Expert Locksmith and EGO's bow bonus with EGO's conditions (bow talents are `ModAttackDamage` on `WeapTypeBow` and never touch a staff spell). **NPCs** (`tools/00-scan-overriders.py` = reusable master-aware binary scan of the live load order → `overriders.json`; `tools/04-rebuild-npcs.py`): Ordo Legionis (Muskets of the Enderal, Nexus 525 — ships its OWN `OrdoLegionisMuskets.esp`, replacing the Skyrim one by filename; same FormIDs) overrides 95 NPCs, the flintlock pack 17, Enderal SE - Crossbows 8 of Ordo's, all as **base-Enderal copies** that reverted EGO's levels/skills/perks/AI/outfits on 82+10. 111 rebuilt = previous non-gun winner verbatim (EGO 92, FS 9, Skyrim 7, our UCR patch 2, EGO-Kata 1) + each gun mod's inventory delta vs the record it copied (Crossbows wins its 8, so Ordo's delta is skipped there); every second hostile musket NPC (Bandit/Nehrimes/Soeldner, sorted) gets a low pistol (Flintlock/Ancient ≤ level 10, else Priest's) + 5–10 bullets instead; 6 non-human carriers (Steel Watcher, Pahtira Centurion, skeleton miner, Miskahmur, Lyguria, Navigator) drop the pistol via a death list instead of wielding it; the porter's 7 deleted-pistol entries are dropped; orphan `02DE35:FS` "Gravewarden" (not a record in FS) skipped. **Sound** (`tools/02`): Ordo's own sounds; only `OLM_WPNBiggunFire 0197F7` / `OLM_WPNBlunderbussFire 00F5B8` re-pointed from the stub `01686B:Dawnguard.esm` output model to `10E425:Skyrim.esm`. **DAR Rifles** (`tools/05`): its anims require `IsEquippedRightHasKeyword("DAR Rifles.esp" | 0x800-0x802)`; the `_KID.ini` (archive root) adds `WeapTypeRifleBoltAction` to Ordo's 14 muskets; the separate ESL hides its 54 modern-gun recipes (AK-47…blasters) with `Smithing >= 1000`, no Items (originals reference Dawnguard). **Holster: tried and reverted 2026-10-12.** Pistol meshes carry `Prn = SHIELD` (sheathed on the back); copies re-pointed at XPMSSE's `WeaponDaggerBackHip` did not give the wanted result in game, so they were removed — the curator places them with **Immersive Equipment Displays** instead. (PyNifly 29.1 cannot write `NiStringExtraData`, `NYI ... type 64`; the edit was a header string-table rewrite.) **Wheeler icons** (`tools/03`): `SKSE/Plugins/wheeler/resources/icons_custom/FID_<plugin>_0X<local FormID, no padding>.svg` (per-keyword variant `KWD_<EditorID>.svg`), curator's art. Masters: Skyrim, Update, FS, EGO, flintlock, Ordo, Crossbows — **load after Enderal SE - Crossbows**. Verified in game: reload/out-of-ammo/soul-gem block.
