## What it does

ALL magic compat/balance patches for the list live in this one plugin. Currently: **Apocalypse cost-ladder repricing** — 175 overrides of the book-taught Apocalypse player spells (their live costs ran 4–7× EGO's ladder: FF tier medians 56/83/175/393/720 vs EGO's 30/53/76/97/124). Mechanism: the spells are ALL auto-calc, so the engine reprices them from effects at runtime and a bare `BaseCost` override does nothing — each override adds `ManualCostCalc` + a hand `BaseCost` (EGO's own archetype, 78/81 of its Kata patch overrides). New cost = per-(tier, FF/conc) ratio onto EGO medians, from `src/MagicPatches/tools/01-build-cost-table.py`; YAML generated by `02-apply-cost-table.ps1` from `reference/mods/Apocalypse`, then `03-retune-fever.ps1` retunes the heals' Arcane Fever tax (Wild Healing 5→1.5 to EGO's flat burst rate, Resurgence 5→4 and King's Heart 12→5 to Enderal's 78-HP-per-point / Panacea precedents; Healing Blossom's 8 was already on the rule). **Regeneration order is 02 then 03** — never hand-edit the Spells folder. Also one MGEF override, NOT script-generated (the `MagicEffects/` folder survives regeneration): `_00E_IncreaseArcaneFeverFFSelf 11A4B6:Skyrim.esm`, EGO's record forwarded verbatim except its fever description — EGO baked the literal `<1,5>%` into the shared effect's text (safe for EGO, whose every user is 1.5), so all four Apocalypse heals displayed "1,5%"; restored base Enderal's `<mag>` token so each spell shows its real fever. Plus **five LVLI distribution-list merges** from `04-merge-distribution-lists.py`: the four `_00E_SpellBooksLoot*` lists (winner = the EGO-Kata patch, which dropped Apocalypse's 8 loot injections each) and `02E_Scrolls 0905A5` (winner was Apocalypse itself, whose override reverted EGO's rebalance — renamed EditorID, `ChanceNone` 0.5→0.15, entry trims — while adding its 4 scrolls). Each = deliberate winner's record verbatim + Apocalypse's additions re-appended. **Weapon-enchantment ×2** from `08-scale-weapon-enchants.py` (regenerates the whole `ObjectEffects/` folder — never hand-edit it): 30 ENCH overrides, every damage/absorb effect on an enchantment a weapon carries set to `2 × max(base Enderal, live)` — undoes EGO's cuts (Boneripper's `EnchWeaponAbsorbHealth02` 3→2 under EGO, now 6) without weakening the few EGO raised (Winter's Edge 13→26); riders (slow, turn undead, paralysis, fear, crit dummies) untouched. Nothing else scales these: EGO's `XionEnchantmentBuff`/`XionEnchantDouble` are `ModEnchantmentPower` (player-crafted only) and `XionMagicDamage` skips `XionWeaponEnch`. They were auto-calc, so each override also gets `NoAutoCalc` + the cost charged today (CK formula, matches 111/113 base-Enderal stored costs; uses EGO's winning MGEF BaseCost — EGO cut `EnchAbsorbHealthFFContact` 40→17) so hits per charge do not change. **Ownership split: Magic Patches owns these five LVLIs; `KataFixes` owns the three vendor chests — neither plugin may touch the other's records.** Since 2026-08-27 also the **Triumvirate cost-ladder repricing + fever tax** (the Triumvirate — Enderal Conversion v1.0.0 deliberately ships Enai's costs untouched, and its master FF tier ran ~10× EGO's ladder: live medians 46/82/168/356/1207): 75 overrides from `05-build-triumvirate-cost-table.py` (tier from Enai's own `TVR_<Arch>_<School><Tier>_` EditorID encoding — known outlier `TVR_Cleric_R025_Spell_Aura_1` carries a shared skill-75 proc) + `06-apply-triumvirate-cost-table.ps1` + `07-add-triumvirate-fever.ps1`, which taxes its three self-heals: Aura of Vigor gets EGO's whole Boon fever block copied verbatim (`106EA4` 0.25/s + the two Mental-perk-conditioned `0083D3:EGO` variants — the magnitude is NOT tunable, `106EA4`'s description bakes in "0,25% per second"), Mass Immortality `11A4B6`@5 (King's Heart/Panacea ceiling), Spirit of the Sun `11A4B6`@10. Drains, fortifies and Aura of Thorns' scripted release-heal stay untaxed, documented in 07's header. **Regeneration order: 02→03 (Apocalypse), 06→07 (Triumvirate) — the two pairs share the Spells folder and each deletes only its own mod's suffix.** **`halfCostPerk` is kept, not cleared**: 274/278 of EGO's own player spells carry the vanilla half-cost perks, so they are live in Enderal's cost system. Masters now eight: base three + `KataPUMBSpellPack.esp` + EGO + Apocalypse + `EGO - KataPUMB Spell Package.esp` + `Triumvirate - Mage Archetypes.esp` — **which forces Magic Patches to load AFTER Triumvirate (319 in the Alpha 0.3 order, i.e. after our old slot 292); move it when deploying**

## Warlock Destruction → Entropy (2026-10-13)

Triumvirate's five Warlock "Destruction" spells (Eldritch Blast, Balefire, Witch Bolt, Cloudkill,
Hurl Into Sinistra) now sit under **Entropy**, not Elementalism. Generated by
`tools/09-reschool-warlock-destruction.ps1`. **Regeneration order is now 06 → 07 → 09**: 06 rewrites
the Spells folder, and 09 re-applies its five `HalfCostPerk` edits. 09 is safe to re-run on its own.

**Why.** Lore: Elementalism is environment-drawn fire, earth, water and air. These spells are raw,
magic and poison damage plus spirit-binding, which is Entropy's necromancy
([`../enderal/magic-schools.md`](../enderal/magic-schools.md)). Perks: every Elementalist
damage/duration perk keys `MagicDamageFire/Frost/Shock` or `MagicRune`, and these effects carry
none of them, so under Elementalism they only ever got the cost discounts. Now the whole Warlock kit
(damage, summons and curses) lives in the Sinistrope tree. **[verified]**

| Records | Count | Change (everything else verbatim from `reference/mods/Triumvirate`) |
|---|---:|---|
| SPEL | 5 | `HalfCostPerk` Destruction&lt;tier&gt; → Conjuration&lt;tier&gt; (`0F2CA7`/`0C44BB`/`0C44BC`/`0C44BD`/`0C44BE`), so Sinistrope's "… of the Dark Arts" discounts apply |
| MGEF | 7 | `MagicSkill: Destruction` → `Conjuration` (= Entropy). All 7 are used **only** by these spells and their staff enchantments |
| BOOK | 5 | tome mesh `SpellTomeDestructionLowPoly.nif` → `SpellTomeConjurationLowPoly.nif`, inventory art `02FBB5` → `02FBB6` (matches Triumvirate's Warlock summon tomes and Enderal's Entropy tomes) |
| WEAP | 4 | staff `Data.Skill: Destruction` → `Conjuration` (Hurl has no staff) |

Deliberately untouched, with the evidence:

- **The 4 staff enchantments** point only at the MGEFs above, so they inherit the new school.
- **Distribution.** Tome and staff lists for all three Warlock schools feed the same FS merchants (`BashHole`, `SisterEnvy`,
  `Wildmage_Undercity` `_CustomMerchandise`), so nothing needs to move.
- **No scrolls exist.** The shared vanilla `Stagger 0153D3` and the school-less Bind Spirit/Prey MGEFs are unchanged.
- **Scripts.** `TVR_Branded_Script`, `TVR_Damnation_Script` and `TVR_SoundAndImod_Script` (decompiled from Triumvirate's BSA)
  read no skill or school. The Enderal Patch's loose `TVR_PopulateSpellBooks_Script` only starts the conversion quest.
- **Conflicts.** Only `Triumvirate - Mage Archetypes.esp` and this plugin contain these EditorIDs
  among the list's installed plugins.

Verified: deserializes clean under Spriggit 0.40, and a serialize round-trip of the built `.esp`
reproduces all 21 records byte-for-byte. **Tested in game 2026-10-13 (profile `Alpha 0.5`): works.** Not checked in xEdit.

## Shadow Mage Destruction → Entropy (2026-10-13)

The same move for Triumvirate's five Shadow Mage "Destruction" spells: Draining Touch, Shadow's
Wrath (Azra's Wrath upstream), Nightblade, Draining Shroud and Draining Mist. Generated by
`tools/10-reschool-shadow-destruction.ps1`. **Regeneration order is now 06 → 07 → 09 → 10.**

**Why.** They are absorb-health/magicka drains and raw magic damage, with no fire/frost/shock
keywords, so no Elementalist damage perk ever applied. EGO's own Sinistrope cost perks call
Entropy "*Schattenzauber*" (shadow spells). The Shadow Mage is now Thaumaturge (Mentalism mobility) +
Sinistrope (Entropy damage, Psionics stealth). Its Alteration and Illusion sets stay put. The
reasoning is in [`../enderal/magic-schools.md`](../enderal/magic-schools.md).

The script re-derives this set from the reference and throws if it drifts |

## Apocalypse re-schooling (2026-10-15)

Eleven Apocalypse spells move to the discipline their effects belong to. Generated by
`tools/11-reschool-apocalypse.ps1`. **Apocalypse regeneration order is now 02 → 03 → 11**: 02
rewrites the Spells folder, and 11 re-applies its eleven `HalfCostPerk` edits. 11 is safe to re-run.

| From → to | Spells |
|---|---|
| Mentalism → Elementalism (Alteration → Destruction) | Talons of Vyn, Raise Wall, Undermine, Entomb, Thrumming Stone, Control Weather |
| Mentalism → Entropy (Alteration → Conjuration) | Wither, Prepare for Adventure |
| Psionics → Entropy (Illusion → Conjuration) | Blood for Blood, Pull From Eternity, Magicka Void |

**Why.** Lore only, from [`../enderal/magic-schools.md`](../enderal/magic-schools.md). Earth and weather
are Elementalism's ("fire, earth, water and air"); Mentalism is hardened air and telekinetic impulse.
Withering, conjured gear, corpses and ghosts are Entropy's. Psionics is illusion in the victim's
head, and these three do real, lasting harm or work through the dead. The review also proposed
moving 19 death/disease/drain spells from Light Magic to Entropy. **That was declined on purpose:
the list keeps offensive Light Magic.** Don't re-propose it.

| Records | Count | Change (everything else verbatim from `reference/mods/Apocalypse`) |
|---|---:|---|
| SPEL | 11 | `HalfCostPerk` to the same tier of the new school |
| MGEF | 18 | `MagicSkill` → `Destruction` (11) / `Conjuration` (7). Includes the procs: Talons' hazard spell `097A50` (explosion `097A47` → hazard `097A45`) and Wither's stacking debuff `083FCB`. The script re-derives each group's set from the reference and throws if it drifts |
| BOOK | 11 | the new school's tome mesh + inventory art (`02FBB5` Destruction / `02FBB6` Conjuration) |
| WEAP | 3 | staff `Data.Skill`: Undermine, Entomb (→ Destruction), Wither (→ Conjuration) |

Deliberately untouched, with the evidence:

- **The 4 proc SPELs' `HalfCostPerk`.** Procs are never paid for. The school-less MGEFs on the chains
  (Undermine procs, `PullFromEternity_Ab`, the shared `AbilityFX 006FB1`) stay as they are.
- **The 10 scrolls and 3 staff ENCHs.** Their school comes from the MGEFs above. Every moved MGEF is
  used only by its own spell, scroll and staff enchantment (`arch-docs/magic` `usedBy`).
- **Distribution.** The `ZP_Apoc_Tomes_R*` lists are split by tier, not by school.
- **Scripts.** `WB_Undermine_Script`, `WB_BloodForBlood_Script` and `WB_ManaVoid_Script` hardcode
  `Game.AdvanceSkill("Alteration"/"Illusion")`. That's inert in Enderal (no skill-use advancement),
  so they aren't recompiled. No other script on the chains reads a skill (decompiled from the BSA).
- **Perks.** No Apocalypse perk keys on these spells' school. Its `ModSpellMagnitude` entries scale
  off the Alteration *actor value*, not school membership.
- **Conflicts.** No other list plugin overrides these records. The reference matches the winning
  `.esp` (the `Zenderal - Apocalypse NAVI Fix` hotfix) except for its dropped NAVI record.

Verified: builds clean (355 records), round-trips record-for-record. **Not yet tested in game.**
