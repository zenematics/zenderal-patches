## What it does

**The third-person staff draw never showed — the staff popped into the hand.** OAR's animation log showed `StaffRight_Equip` activating (original), then `MRh_and_MLh_Equip` + `MLh_Equip` activating right after, both `[Replaced]` by Smooth Magic Casting Animation 4.2's legacy DAR folder `_CustomConditions/996`, whose condition is always true (`IsActorBase(Player) OR NOT IsActorBase(Player)`), so its magic equip plays over the staff draw. Dynamic Random Spell Idle replaces the same clips too: its `Base Equip` excludes a right-hand staff (`IsEquippedType 8`, negated; 8 = staff in OAR as in DAR, cf. Dynamic Sprint's `0 Base Staff Left`), but its `Random Equip 1–3` (priority 8210022–24) do NOT, and a second log (2026-10-13) showed `Random Equip 2` winning the staff draw. **v1 (vanilla magic clips) did NOT fix it — verified in game 2026-10-13:** OAR's log showed `MRh_and_MLh_Equip` and `MLh_Equip` played by this submod with the vanilla clips, and the staff still popped in. With a staff in the right hand and a spell in the left, the graph runs `StaffRight_Equip` *and* the full-upper-body dual-magic equip, which hides the staff draw even in vanilla form. **v2:** one OAR submod `Staff draw in magic equip slots` (`IsEquippedType 8` right hand, priority 8300000) whose `mrh_and_mlh_equip.hkx` and `mrh_equip.hkx` are the vanilla `staffright_equip.hkx`, plus vanilla `mlh_equip`; unequip clips are left to the list (vanilla has no staff sheathe clip). The vanilla magic equip clips carry no annotations; `staffright_equip` carries only `weaponDraw`. Generated by `src/StaffDrawFix/tools/01-build-staff-draw-fix.py` from `Skyrim - Animations.bsa` (not committed — Bethesda's clips). First person is unaffected (IC-VanillaAnims, priority 99999999). v2 built 2026-10-13, not yet verified in game. An earlier test ran on a CRDW cache that did not contain the mod, so OAR never saw the submod: **a new OAR folder stays invisible until CRDW rebuilds**, while a loose mesh at a known path does not need the cache, which is why the Dragon Priest staff swap worked in the same session.
